# Firearms Act - Handling Utterances During Interview
Act: Firearms Act
Chapter: 16:01
Section: 4
URL: https://www.legalaffairs.gov.tt/

When a suspect makes an utterance during an interview, officers must avoid obtaining informal admissions outside the required legal safeguards.

Any substantive statement that may be relied upon should be reduced into a Statement under Caution process according to TTPS procedure and evidential rules.

Further questioning should proceed only after procedural safeguards are applied and recorded in the case documentation.
