# TTPS Standing Orders - Recording and Authentication of Statements
Act: TTPS Standing Orders
Chapter: Operational Orders
Section: Interview Procedure 5.3
URL: https://example.org/ttps-standing-orders/interview-procedure

When a person under interview makes an incriminating utterance, interviewing officers must pause the interview process and transition to a formal Statement under Caution procedure.

The statement should be read back to the suspect, and where the procedure requires authentication, the suspect's statement is to be authenticated before a Justice of the Peace.

No further substantive questioning should continue until the required caution and authentication steps are completed and recorded.
