# TTPS Standing Orders - Interview Procedure
Act: TTPS Standing Orders
Chapter: Police Procedure
Section: Interview and Utterance Handling
URL: https://example.tt/ttps-standing-orders/interview-procedure

When a suspect makes an utterance during a police interview, officers must pause substantive questioning
and transition to a Statement under Caution process before continuing with further interview questions.

The suspect should be informed of the caution, and the statement should be recorded in accordance with
standing orders, preserving the suspect's exact words and context.

Where required by procedure, the statement and process must be authenticated by a Justice of the Peace
or other authorized officer before interview continuation.
